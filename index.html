Viditel Modem Emulator

Install the latest release directly from your browser on your ESP32-WROOM-DA.

Step 1: Connect your ESP32-WROOM-DA to your computer via USB.

Step 2: Click the button and select the correct COM port.


Your browser does not support Web Serial. Please use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Opera on a desktop computer.
Does the installer get stuck on "Connecting..."?
Press and hold the BOOT button on your ESP32-WROOM-DA as soon as you click connect, and do not release it until the erasing/writing process begins.
